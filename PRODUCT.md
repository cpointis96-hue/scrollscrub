# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Vite static site with Canvas and GSAP ScrollTrigger, chosen for deterministic scrub playback without framework overhead.

## Users

Visitors encountering an experimental luxury-fragrance campaign and exploring its product film through scroll.

## Product Purpose

NOIR 01 is a fictional haute-parfumerie campaign prototype. Its success is a silent, cinematic product experience in which scrolling precisely assembles the bottle.

## Positioning

The experience treats scroll as a deterministic film head for a single object, rather than as page decoration or a conventional product landing page.

## Operating Context

Desktop and mobile web, with a high-quality visual sequence, real HTML editorial copy, and an accessible reduced-motion alternative.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Locked camera, physical product assembly, Canvas-rendered image sequence, and reversible scroll playback.
- No subscription, purchase, plan change, or credit purchase is authorized.
- The project must preserve the Flow master video and have a repeatable FFmpeg extraction script.
- Assumptions inferred from the explicit brief: no deployment target or product audience beyond campaign visitors was specified.

## Brand Commitments

NOIR 01; Parisian haute parfumerie, industrial precision, contemporary art, and architectural minimalism. The supplied warm-black, ivory, and restrained champagne palette is binding. The user cited Magic UI, Aceternity, AI SDK Elements, Beautiful UI, BeUI, Rare UI, and Transitions.dev only as a benchmark for craft and motion, not as a visual style to copy.

## Evidence on Hand

No existing product assets or factual claims. Source imagery and the Flow video must be created for this prototype; no testimonials, metrics, or external claims may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- The bottle is the hero; interface chrome recedes.
- Every motion communicates mechanical precision.
- The film remains controlled in either scroll direction.
- Mobile and reduced motion are first-class experiences.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Real HTML text, keyboard-visible controls, contrast checks, responsive reflow, and a static reduced-motion alternative are required.
